Hi! You can commission me to draw furries and other creatures.
Here are some important details, please read them:

  • I do not do commissions for minors (sorry!)
  • I like to be a bit experimental when drawing something. You probably shouldn't commission me to draw things requiring lots of accuracy (like a refsheet).
  • I'm a bit of a procrastinator, so you should avoid commissioning me with a deadline in mind (like for a birthday present). I will try my best, though! Feel free to check in with me if I haven't given an update in a few days.
  • I will also try my best to draw your character accurately, and I'll gladly tweak lines or colors. However, please don't ask me to draw a different pose or camera angle halfway through the process, as that is essentially restarting the commission.
    (If you want this process to go more smoothly, providing visual direction at the start is very helpful)
  • If you want to commission NSFW art, details can be found on this page.

Things I like drawing:

Anything involving my sona!
